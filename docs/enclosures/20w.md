## What it does

Interior 184 × 118 × 55 mm; base prints at 216.8 × 122.8 × 58, lid at 204.8 × 122.8 × 9.4, and
both together on one A1 plate (`20W-enclosure-plate.stl`, 216.8 × 250.6). On
one line, left to right: the C14 lying down in the left wall (toward the front), the S-20-5 with
its terminals facing the front wall (28 mm of terminal space), and the DIKAVS perfboard turned
90° to run parallel to the PSU, against the back wall. Two round JST-SM holes through the floor
in front of the perfboard.
Slide-lock lid with a zip-tie lock on the right wall; three mounting tabs on the short walls.

## Decisions

- **Layout is the owner's (2026-10-08):** inlet on the left short wall, PSU "wires pointing
  down" (terminals to the front), perfboard beside and parallel to it, LED exits in the floor
  ("on the bottom") next to the perfboard. With the terminals to the front, AC (L, N, ⏚) is on the
  left — toward the inlet — and DC on the right — toward the perfboard, so no wires cross; the
  side rule confirms it. This needed PSUs that face the front/back walls, added to the builder
  for this enclosure ([../enclosure/psu_mount.md](../enclosure/psu_mount.md)).
- **Inlet at u = 36, toward the front**: its rear faces the PSU's left side close to the
  terminals, and its wire zone (x 35–60) leads straight forward into the terminal space. 36 is
  the closest its 69 mm flange gets to the front corner.
- **PSU at x = 60** = inlet body (35) + its wire zone (25). **Perfboard 5 mm right of the PSU**
  and 3 mm from the back wall (like the PSU), leaving 34 mm in front of it for the LED holes.
  **Changed 2026-10-08:** was 20 mm from the front wall; the round 20 mm holes and their anchors
  need 34.
- **Height 55 mm, set by the inlet's flange nuts** (same reason as [100w.md](100w.md)); the
  S-20-5 alone (34 + 3.5 pads) would allow ~45. Upright, the inlet would need ~66.
- **LED exits in the floor strip between the perfboard and the front wall**: round 20 mm holes
  at y = 12 (2 mm off the front wall), x = 140 and 166 as before, so the board is drilled
  straight through them (owner, 2026-10-08; sizes in
  [../enclosure/wall_openings.md](../enclosure/wall_openings.md)). Zip-tie anchors 16 mm behind
  each hole (`anchor_at`), between it and the perfboard, on the pigtails' path; the default spot
  (18 mm) would touch the board. **Changed 2026-10-08:** were 15.6 × 10.2 slots at y = 10,
  anchors 10 mm to their right; the owner could not drill the board through a slot.
  **Changed 2026-10-08:** first built in the front wall — "bottom" had been read as the bottom
  of the top view; the owner meant the floor.
- **Base and lid fit one plate** (owner, 2026-10-08). That took three changes: mounting tabs
  moved to the short walls (the box is short, so long-wall tabs were the only thing making it
  deep), the zip-tie lugs moved to the right short wall (u = 59), and the width went from 122 to
  118 — terminal space 30 → 28 (overriding the library value) and the gap behind the PSU 5 → 3.
  The plate is 250.6 mm deep, 3.4 mm inside the 254 mm usable.
- **Three tabs: left wall at u = 100 (clear of the inlet flange), right wall at u = 20 and 98.**
- **Vents in the back wall behind the PSU's far end** (3 mm air gap), plus lid slots over the PSU. The front
  wall is taken by the terminal wiring.

## Still to check (photo estimates, not measured)

- S-20-5 bottom holes: on the AC-side long edge (owner); ~4.5 mm in from it and ~5.5 mm from
  the terminal end are photo estimates. Centre spacing 67.6 mm assumes "64.6 mm between hole
  edges" meant inner edges of ~3 mm holes. Slots allow ±2 mm along the length.
- S-20-5 height (34 mm assumed from the side photo).
- The label lists 100–120 V / 200–220 V input: if the unit has a voltage selector, it must be on
  the 220 V setting for 230 V mains.

## Gotchas

- **The board under the box must be drilled at the LED holes** (owner mounts it on a wooden
  board and runs the LED wires through it, 2026-10-08): 18 mm, straight through each 20 mm hole,
  with the box screwed down and empty. The BOM also gives the hole centres from the left
  mounting tab.
- Each JST-SM contact is rated 3 A; the PSU's 4 A needs both exits sharing the load.
