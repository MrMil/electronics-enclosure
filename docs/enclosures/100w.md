# 100W (and -rj45, -leds-on-floor)

Path: [docs](../README.md) › [enclosures](README.md) › **100w**
Parent: [README.md](README.md)
**Code:** `enclosures/100W/enclosure.yaml`, `enclosures/100W-rj45/enclosure.yaml`, `enclosures/100W-leds-on-floor/enclosure.yaml`
**Covers:** the first enclosure in its three variants: layout decisions and what the owner should
still check.

## What it does

Interior 244 × 186 × 55 mm; base prints at 248.8 × 218.8 × 58, lid at 252.8 × 202.8 × 9.4
(A1 bed 256 with a 2 mm margin; the lid has 1.2 mm to spare in length). Across the width:

- **Front:** a 10 mm channel with four printed wire loops on the front wall, along the PSU's DC side.
- **Middle:** the S-100-5, terminals toward the left wall (35 mm of terminal space).
- **Back band (AC side):** the C14 lying down in the back wall right behind the L/N/FG
  terminals; the perfboard further right; four JST-SM slots in the back wall beside it. In
  `-rj45`, the RJ45 in the right wall level with the perfboard. In `-leds-on-floor`, no slots in
  the back wall: four round JST-SM holes in the floor right of the perfboard instead.

Low-voltage wires go from the DC terminals into the front channel, along the PSU, round its right
end (two more loops) and into the back band to the perfboard. Tool-free slide-lock lid with a
zip-tie lock on the front wall; four mounting tabs.

## Decisions

- **Inlet and perfboard share the back (AC-side) long wall; low voltage goes round the far end.**
  Owner design (2026-10-07), replacing a centred PSU with a 60 mm mains band that was mostly
  empty: width 239 → 186, and the tabs fit again. Inlet wires reach the AC terminals directly;
  DC wires never pass the terminal end on the AC side
  ([../enclosure/wire_routing.md](../enclosure/wire_routing.md)). The perfboard starts 39 mm
  beyond the inlet (the rule needs 20).
  **Changed 2026-10-07:** previously the PSU sat in the middle with the inlet behind it and the
  perfboard in front, because the rule only allowed low voltage on the DC side.
- **Terminal space 35 mm (library default 40).** The inlet's wires now arrive from behind rather
  than the side; 40 would push the lid past the bed. 35 fits ferrules and a 14 AWG bend.
- **Front and far-end channels 10 mm** = 8.6 mm wall loop + 1 mm; the front one doubles as the air
  path past the PSU's front face to the front-wall vents. The 2 mm saved when the loops shrank
  went to the terminal space (length is bed-limited) and to a narrower box.
- **Height 55 mm, set by the inlet's flange nuts.** Lying down (`rotate: 90`, flange 50 mm tall
  instead of 69) the C14's two bolts are stacked 39.8 mm apart; their nuts must clear the floor
  and the lid lip, which needs 55 (inlet at z = 24). The PSU alone would allow ~50: it sits at
  41.5 on its pads, leaving 13.5 mm of air under the lid vents.
  **Changed 2026-10-08:** was 52 with the inlet at z = 22.2, which put the lower nut below the
  floor and the upper one into the lid lip; the validator did not check nuts then.
- **C14 at u = 37 on the back wall**, the closest its 69 mm flange gets to the corner; its rear
  faces the terminal space.
- **Zip-tie lock on the front wall at u = 70**, between the front-left and middle tongues, for
  Midburn ([../enclosure/lid_closure.md](../enclosure/lid_closure.md)). Front, so it is on the
  low-voltage side and reachable when the box sits against a wall by its back.
- **Back-wall tongue at u = 88, not 22**: the inlet flange reaches the lock zone (auto-shifted).
- **Tabs: front at u = 40 and 204, back at u = 100 and 204** — the back-left tab would hit the
  inlet flange.
- **Vents along the front channel and the right wall beside the PSU's end**, plus lid slots over
  the PSU.
- **Two enclosures, one layout.** `-rj45` `extends` the base; the base keeps the RJ45's 60 mm
  cable zone free (perfboard ends at x = 181, zone starts at 184). RJ45 at u = 138.5, level with
  the perfboard's centre.
- **`-leds-on-floor`: the four LED exits as a 2 × 2 grid of round floor holes at x 196/222,
  y 126/160** (owner asked for floor exits, no RJ45, 2026-10-08). That is the space `100W` keeps
  free for `-rj45`'s patch cable, so nothing else moves and the parts mount exactly as in
  `100W`; it is 5 mm from the perfboard's right edge, on the DC side of the mains separation, and
  clear of the far-end LV route (x 234–244, y < 108). The 58 × 75 mm space is too narrow for a
  row of four 20 mm holes, and the 20 mm strip behind the perfboard too shallow for any. Zip-tie
  anchors at the default spot behind each hole. The drill sizes and why the holes are round:
  [../enclosure/wall_openings.md](../enclosure/wall_openings.md).
- **Named by the owner** after the PSU's power; STLs are `<name>-enclosure-base.stl` /
  `-lid.stl` (see [../pipeline/output.md](../pipeline/output.md)).

## Still to check (from datasheets, not measured)

- S-100-5: bottom holes 120 mm apart lengthwise, 80 mm across, nearest 62 mm from the terminal
  end (Mean Well S-100F case 902). Nothing inside should sit within 3 mm of the base plate above
  a bottom hole.
- JST pigtail housings pass the 15.6 × 10.2 mm slot (print `test-prints/jst-sm-3pin-coupon.stl` to check);
  in `-leds-on-floor`, that they pass an 18 mm hole drilled in the board.
- C14 flange overhang (~11 mm) and body depth (assumed 35 of the 60 mm).
- RJ45 outside flange (assumed ≤ the 29 mm nut).

## Gotchas

- Four JST-SM exits carry at most 12 A (3 A per contact) of the PSU's 20 A.
- The lid has 1.2 mm to spare on the A1 bed; any extra length needs a shorter `lid.tab_pull` or a
  bigger printer.
- Feed the JST pigtails out through the slots before screwing the perfboard down (20 mm zone).
- `-leds-on-floor`: drill the mounting board through the floor holes with the box screwed down
  and still empty — a mounted perfboard is 5 mm from the left-hand holes and in the drill's way.
